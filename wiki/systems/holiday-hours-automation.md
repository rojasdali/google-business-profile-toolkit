# Holiday Hours Automation

## Summary
The system that proactively schedules special holiday business hours (e.g. Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Eve, Memorial Day) across Google Maps profiles weeks in advance.

## Key Facts
- Prevents customer frustration caused by unverified "Hours might differ" Google Maps warnings.
- Supports both modified schedule hours and full day closures (`is_closed=True`).
- Syncs directly to Google My Business special hours endpoints via API payload formatting.
