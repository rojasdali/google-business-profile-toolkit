# NAP Consistency

## Summary
The local SEO standard requiring absolute consistency across Name, Address, and Phone number between Google Business Profile, web pages, schema markup, and external citations.

## Key Facts
- Prevents rank dispersion caused by divergent phone formats or suite address inconsistencies.
- Cross-references location metadata with canonical business constants.
- Flags mismatches automatically during audit checks.
